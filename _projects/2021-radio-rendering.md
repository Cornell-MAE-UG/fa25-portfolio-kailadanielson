This semester in my class MAE 3780 Mechatronics, I collaborated on a team of three to design and build a competitive robot, FAAFO, for a cube-collection challenge. The robot was designed to efficiently gather, transport, and protect cubes during one-minute matches against other student-built robots. This project was a culmination of our semester-long study of common electronic components and circuits, C++ programming, and the use of an Arduino Uno in engineering applications.

I primarily contributed to the assembly and testing process, helping to fabricate and construct the robot, integrate our electronic and mechanical components, and evaluate performance through iterative testing and troubleshooting. Our robot performed strongly during competition and made it to the quarterfinals. This experience strengthened my skills in mechanical assembly, problem solving, teamwork, and hands-on engineering design.
